3. Core game rules

01

Read the arrow path

An arrow may be cleared when its path from the arrowhead to the edge is not blocked by another active arrow.

02

Blocked taps cost a heart

In normal gameplay, tapping a blocked arrow may reduce your remaining hearts.

03

Clear every arrow

A puzzle is completed after all active arrows have been successfully removed.

04

Boosters are optional

Hints, Route Scanner, and Power Blast may assist gameplay but are not required for a guaranteed-solvable board.

Puzzle layouts, difficulty, rewards, and balancing may vary by level or App version. We may adjust game rules to improve fairness, stability, performance, or user experience.

  • The reward is granted after the advertising SDK confirms that the reward condition was met.
  • The App currently permits a maximum of seven rewarded ads per calendar day.
  • A user must generally wait at least five minutes after one rewarded ad before requesting another.
  • The seven-per-day limit and five-minute cooldown apply across rewarded placements. The Unity version offers extra hearts and hints; older versions may offer bonus coins. The Unity defaults also limit an attempt to one rewarded extra heart and three rewarded hints.
  • Ad availability is not guaranteed and may depend on internet access, consent status, location, demand, account review, and third-party systems.
  • Closing an ad early, losing network access, or an ad-provider failure may prevent a reward when completion is not confirmed.

During a timed event, Arrowza pauses the event timer and game audio while a supported rewarded ad is displayed and resume it when the ad closes.

Interstitial ads may appear at a completed main-level transition when you choose Next Level. Default limits skip the first three completed levels, use approximately three completions between opportunities, enforce at least 150 seconds between interstitials, and allow at most eight per rolling hour. Interstitials are suppressed after a recent rewarded ad and while victory effects/audio are playing. They are not shown on launch, during a puzzle, after failure, on restart or in settings. If no ad is ready, gameplay continues without waiting for an ad to load.

Watching ads never guarantees a monetary benefit. Virtual rewards have no cash value. Ad availability, consent choices and SDK/account status can affect whether an offer is available.
